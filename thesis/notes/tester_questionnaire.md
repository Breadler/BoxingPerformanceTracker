it's fresh. Takes ~5 minutes. Answers feed §5.7 (user-centered evaluation).

## Part 2 — Open questions

1. Was there anything on screen you didn't understand or weren't sure how to
   read?

2. Did anything in the results look wrong or surprise you (a punch count,
   a graph, the skeleton tracking)? Describe what happened.

3. If you were reviewing your own training footage, what's the single most
   useful thing this app could show you that it doesn't right now?

4. Anything else — confusing, annoying, broken, or that you liked?

---

_Note for the record (n=3): with only three testers this isn't a
statistically powered usability study — treat scores as directional and lean
on the open answers and the observed failures from the device/scenario log
for the actual write-up, rather than averaging the Likert scores as if they
were significant._
